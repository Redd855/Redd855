### Adrenaline Rush

**GMTK Game Jam | July 2026**

* Developed a **2.5D high-score beat 'em up** within a **4-day game jam**, designing a combat loop centered around maintaining combos and maximizing score.
* Implemented AI for **three enemy types: Grunts, Gunners, and Tank**. They use pathfinding, state-based behaviors, and separation forces to surround the player while preventing enemies from clumping together.
* Designed enemy attack coordination so enemies **reposition around the player and take turns engaging**, creating continuous combat pressure without overwhelming the player with simultaneous attacks.

**Technologies:** Unity, C#, Pathfinding, Finite State Machines
